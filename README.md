# Telstra – Financial and Non-Financial Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on evaluating Telstra's financial performance and non-financial activities, analysing its financial statements, sustainability initiatives, corporate governance, business strategy and competitive position within the Australian telecommunications industry.

## Objectives

- Analyse Telstra's financial performance over three years (2023–2025).
- Evaluate profitability, liquidity, efficiency and debt ratios.
- Examine sustainability practices and greenhouse gas emission reductions.
- Assess corporate governance and customer satisfaction.
- Identify business strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.
- Provide recommendations for financial stability and sustainable growth.

## Methodologies & Frameworks

- Horizontal Financial Analysis
- Financial Ratio Analysis
- SWOT Analysis
- Sustainability and ESG Analysis
- Competitor Analysis
